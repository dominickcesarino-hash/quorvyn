How to use Quorvyn

Everything Quorvyn can do, in one place.

💬 Chat

Type anything in the message bar and press Enter, or tap one of the suggestion cards on the home screen. Under each reply you can Copy it or have it Read aloud. Press Shift + Enter for a new line.

🎙 Talk out loud

Tap Talk in the message bar to have a spoken conversation. Quorvyn listens, answers out loud, then listens again. Tap the orb to interrupt or to say you're done, and tap End to stop. Use ▶ Test voice and the voice menu to pick how Quorvyn sounds (✨ natural voice sounds the most human).

🖥 Show Quorvyn your screen

On a computer, tap the screen button in the message bar and choose a tab or window. While the blue bar shows, every message includes a snapshot so you can ask "what does this mean?" Tap Stop anytime. Not available on iPhone.

🌤 Weather

Tap the weather card on the home screen to pick your city (or use your location) and °F or °C. Then ask things like "do I need a jacket today?"

✉️ Emails and 📅 calendar events

Say "email my coach that I'll be late" or "add study group Tuesday at 4pm." Quorvyn makes a card with buttons like Open in Gmail or Add to Google Calendar. It opens ready to go, and you hit Send or Save yourself.

👤 Your account

Tap Sign in (Google or email) to save your chats and pick a unique @username, a name and a profile picture. In your profile, fill in What should Quorvyn remember about you? and it will use that in every chat. Signing in is optional.

☰ Saved chats

When you're signed in, tap ☰ (top left) to reopen past chats. Tap 🗑 twice to delete one. Tap ＋ (top right) to start a new chat.

🔊 Read replies aloud

Turn it on from your name menu (or the 🔊 button if you're not signed in) and every reply is read out loud.

🛡 Admin (only you see this)

Open Admin from your name menu to see stats, post an announcement, pause Quorvyn, ban or unban accounts, and give or take back OG badges.

🔒 Privacy

Messages, screenshots and voice replies are processed by Google's free Gemini service, which may use them to improve its products, so don't share passwords or private info. Your saved chats are only visible to you.
